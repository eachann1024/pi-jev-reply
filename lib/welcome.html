About Jev, privacy & recovery

With a Jev key, Jev reviews eligible replies before your model edits them. Without one, the extension uses your current model directly.

To enable Jev, set TYPESAFE_API_KEY before starting Pi, or save your key in ~/.config/typesafe/api_key. Never put a key in a chat.

Review sends the draft and a short excerpt of your request to Jev. Editing sends them to your selected model. Failed edits keep the original reply.
